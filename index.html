🛡️ Cyber Security Awareness

1. Protect Your Account

Use strong, unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication.

2. Check Unknown Links

Verify the website address before opening links. Fake websites may try to steal personal information.

3. Keep OTP Private

Never share your password, OTP or recovery codes with anyone.

4. Learn Ethically

Practice security only on your own devices or systems where you have permission.
