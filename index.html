Cyber Safety

Protect yourself and respect other people's privacy.

🔐

Protect Accounts

Use unique passwords and two-factor authentication. Never share OTPs or recovery codes.

🎣

Recognize Phishing

Check website addresses and avoid suspicious links or untrusted login pages.

🧠

Think Before Clicking

Verify unexpected messages and report suspicious online activity.

Practice cybersecurity only with permission. Respect privacy and never access another person's account without authorization.
