STAY SMART ONLINE

Cyber Safety

Protect yourself and respect other people's privacy.

🔐

Protect Accounts

Use unique strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication. Never share OTPs or recovery codes with anyone.

🎣

Recognize Phishing

Always check website addresses carefully. Avoid suspicious links and untrusted login pages.

🧠

Think Before Clicking

Verify unexpected messages. Report any suspicious online activity immediately.

Practice cybersecurity only with proper permission. Respect privacy and never access another person's account without authorization.
